A Family Fragrance Story

Our story began in 2008 at Madargate, Ajmer, when our founder opened a perfume shop with a simple belief: a memorable fragrance should feel personal and welcoming.

Over the years, the shop grew through the trust of its customers and a shared love of fragrance. The founder's son now carries the family business forward, honoring its roots while welcoming a new generation of fragrance lovers.

From fresh and floral notes to warm woods and rich aromas, we bring together scents for everyday moments and special occasions. We are proud to serve the Ajmer community with the same care and personal attention that shaped the shop from the beginning.
